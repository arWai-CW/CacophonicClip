Cacophonic Clip for macOS

1. Install

   Right-click "Install Cacophonic Clip.app" and choose Open, then Open again.

   macOS will say it cannot verify the developer. That is expected: this plug-in
   is not signed by Apple.

   If Open is missing from the menu, double-click it, wait for the warning, then
   go to System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click
   "Open Anyway", and double-click it again.

2. Open your DAW

   After the installer finishes, quit and reopen your DAW (Cmd+Q) so it rescans
   the plug-in list.

   VST3 works in every current DAW.
   Logic also loads it as an audio unit, under Audio Units > External A/AU.


Uninstall

   Drag these two items to the Trash:

     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Cacophonic Clip.vst3
     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Cacophonic Clip.component

   The installer app can be deleted right away, it carries the plug-in inside it.
